How ReDoor works

01

Rescued

Sound doors taken out of houses that were replacing them — bought and brought back to the workshop.

02

Assessed

Every door goes on the bench. Springs, cables, rollers, hinges and tracks all checked, and the work written down.

03

Stripped & cleaned

Years of grime off and back to a clean finish — and repainted in any colour you ask for.

04

Serviced & re-packed

Worn parts replaced, everything adjusted and greased, then a brand-new screw and fixings pack made up.

05

Ready to fit

Graded, priced and listed. It arrives complete — no chasing missing bits.
